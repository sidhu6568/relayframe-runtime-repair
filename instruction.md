# Repair the RelayFrame stream decoder

An edge telemetry gateway delivers RelayFrame v1 over arbitrary byte chunks. The decoder in `/app/relayframe/decoder.py` handles its clean demo but loses or corrupts records when reads split escapes or frames, logical segments arrive out of order, sequence numbers wrap, or corrupt traffic appears. Repair that file. Preserve `StreamDecoder()` and `feed(chunk: bytes) -> list[DecodedRecord]`; each call returns only records completed by that call. Malformed input is dropped without raising, and later valid traffic must still decode. Import `DecodedRecord` from `/app/relayframe/model.py`; do not redefine it.

A wire frame is `0x7e + escaped_body + 0x7e`. Inside a frame, `0x7d 0x5e` represents literal `0x7e`, and `0x7d 0x5d` represents literal `0x7d`. Ignore bytes before the first delimiter and ignore empty frames. A delimiter ends the current candidate and starts the next one. Partial bodies and a pending `0x7d` escape survive across `feed` calls. Any other escape pair, a dangling escape at a delimiter, or an unescaped body longer than 2072 bytes invalidates that candidate through its next delimiter.

The unescaped body contains a big-endian 20-byte `>BBHHIIIH` header: version (must equal 1), flags (only bit 0 is allowed and means zlib compression), `channel_id`, 16-bit `sequence`, `record_id`, absolute payload `offset`, total encoded-record length, and segment payload length. The header is followed by exactly that many payload bytes and a four-byte big-endian CRC32 of header plus payload. Require exact body length, segment payload at most 2048 bytes, total encoded length at most 65536 bytes, and an offset range contained in the total. A zero total is valid only for an uncompressed zero-length record at offset zero. A nonzero total requires a nonempty segment. Validate the entire frame and its CRC before changing replay or assembly state.

Maintain an independent 64-entry replay window per channel using modulo-65536 serial ordering. Relative to the high-water sequence, forward distances 1 through 32767 are newer, zero is a duplicate, and exactly 32768 is invalid. An older unseen sequence is accepted only when it is fewer than 64 positions behind the high-water mark. Corrupt or malformed frames do not consume a sequence number. A structurally valid, CRC-correct frame that passes replay does consume its sequence even when a later assembly conflict or decompression failure prevents output.

Reassemble records by `(channel_id, record_id)` using absolute payload offsets. Segments may be interleaved, reversed, gapped, and overlapping. Flags and total encoded length must agree across a record. Identical overlapping bytes are allowed. Any conflicting overlap or metadata mismatch deletes the prior assembly and rejects the incoming segment: none of the rejected segment's bytes may remain. Keep at most eight incomplete records globally. When a newly accepted segment would leave a ninth incomplete key, evict the record whose most recent accepted segment is oldest. An accepted identical overlap refreshes that record's age. A record completed by its first segment never occupies an incomplete slot.

When every encoded byte is present, remove the assembly and emit one immutable `DecodedRecord(channel_id, record_id, payload)`. For flag 0, the assembled bytes are the payload. For flag 1, decompress the complete assembled value once with zlib; require one finished stream, no trailing data, and at most 262144 decoded bytes. Invalid or oversized compressed data emits nothing and must not disrupt later frames.

The only required artifact is the repaired `/app/relayframe/decoder.py`. Make that module self-contained apart from Python's standard library and `relayframe.model`; added helper files are not transferred to the verifier. The environment is offline. Run `python /app/examples/run_capture.py` to observe the clean public capture before and after your repair.
